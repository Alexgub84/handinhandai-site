Supersedes the "skip the 12 testers" framing wherever the two conflict.

## What changed, in his words

- Israeli groups already exist where people help each other with testers, for free.
  So "we get you past the 12-tester gate" is not a differentiator: the gate is
  already solved socially, and cheaply.
- The real buyer is someone who **built an app with AI only** and now needs help
  **setting it up and publishing it** — not someone shopping for testers.
- He can do the whole job, not just the upload: he can help design the app itself,
  fix it, and publish it. The offer should say so.
- Target: young and inexperienced developers. The page must teach them, and the
  prices must be inside that teaching, not bolted on at the end.
- Pricing needs to be better than the current 590 / 790 / 1,290.

## Consequences for the page

1. The 12-tester explainer stops being the headline argument. It stays as one fact
   among several, because people do search it, but it is no longer the promise.
2. The promise moves to: you built something with AI, it is not a product yet, and
   one experienced developer turns it into one and gets it into the stores.
3. Scope widens from "publishing" to "getting an AI-built app to a shippable state
   and into the stores", which is also what makes the price defensible.
4. The audience is a beginner. Every section must be readable by someone who does
   not know what a certificate, a bundle id or an AAB is, and must teach it in the
   same sentence it uses it.
5. Prices are presented as part of the explanation of the work, not as a table at
   the end of a sales page.

## Pricing and structure — manager recommendation, 2 November 2026

Three independent redesigns, after six pre-mortems, converged on the same shape.
The two that the panel rated best agreed on all three structural points.

**Unanimous, all three seats:** never host a client app on the verified Google
organisation account. The correct number is zero, not a vetted few. The account
carries his own clients' live products; one strike on a stranger's app puts those
at risk. The Android half becomes: the client's own account, and he does the work
inside it, exactly like the Apple half.

**Two of three, and the two rated best:** the free readiness check is killed or
priced. A paid gate before any build is opened is the only thing that stops first
contact from being free engineering, which every pre-mortem named as the primary
cause of death.

**Unanimous:** no three-month inclusive support tail, no monthly retainer. The tail
was the second half of the same leak.

### Prices proposed

| Seat | Gate | One store | Both stores | Extra work |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (joint best) | 790, mandatory, not credited | 2,490 Android / 3,290 Apple | 4,490 | 990/hr |
| B (joint best) | 390, credited if booked in 21 days | 2,290 | 3,490 | 450/hr, 3h block |
| C (ranked last) | free checkbox, then 390 audit | 1,490 / 1,890 | 2,790 | 450/hr, min 2h |

Resulting hourly rates on a realistic job with one rejection loop: 1,006 (A),
540 (B), 455 (C). Against roughly 46 today.

### Manager recommendation

Seat B's shape, at Seat B's numbers: **390 gate credited against the package, 2,290
one store, 3,490 both, 450/hour for engineering in prepaid blocks.** It sits above
the Israeli competitor's floor and below its ceiling, the credited gate keeps the
page's lead flow alive, and the hourly rate is defensible beside consulting.

Volume reality: 2 to 3 jobs a month makes this worth doing. Below 4 jobs in any
trailing 90 days, stop.

### Kill criteria to pre-register

- Blended rate across all jobs under 350 ILS/hour, measured at the 5th client.
- Fewer than 3 paying clients by 31 December 2026.
- Fewer than 4 jobs in any trailing 90 days.
- Immediate stop: any client app on the organisation account, or any Play
  enforcement mail naming an app he does not own.
