The AI answer that renders is a consumer-tech answer, off-topic. Verdict recorded in the map: **no audience under this exact phrasing** — the real pain has no bridge to a searchable Google query as the reader words it.

**Query `ארכיטקטורה נכונה לאפליקציה שנבנתה עם AI` (demand map #10, read 2026-11-25).** The same subject, in the phrasing a developer would use instead of the reader's. **CROWDED**: DevShift, elya-studio.com's `strategy.` subdomain, the-hunters.co.il, with Geektime covering the adjacent "don't let AI be its own QA" angle. No distinct AI-Overview citation card; the overview answered in text. The map's own verdict: crowded on the *technical* term, but **no competitor answers the felt, narrative version of this pain**.

**The surrounding meta-finding (same document).** 18 of 21 tested vibe-coding subjects already carry multiple dated, competent Hebrew guides, from a repeat cast of Israeli AI consultants (elya-studio.com across three subdomains, יהב רובין, Let's AI, tzedek.me, עמית טק, Skills IL, smartup.co.il, spiritt.ai, מור ממן) plus Geektime and mainstream news. ### Who already ranks, and what only this desk can argue

The incumbents found under #10 write the subject as **architecture** — a technical virtue, explained to someone who already accepts that architecture is a thing. Nobody found writes it as **triage**: what to do on the morning the thing stopped moving, in the order a person with no training can actually perform, with the honest answer to "should I just start over".

What only this desk can put on the page: a project that is actually run this way, where the rules the assistant must follow live in a file it reads at the start of every session, and where the rules that matter are enforced by a check that fails the build rather than by anyone remembering them. That is first-hand and inspectable, and it is the one claim in the guide that is not general practice.

### Verdict

**Search: provisional-to-closed, and deliberately published anyway.** The reader's own phrasing reaches the wrong intent (#9, read 2026-11-25); the technical phrasing is crowded by at least three dated Hebrew competitors (#10, same read); and whether any third phrasing bridges the two was never established. **Community: the strongest single signal in the whole Facebook scan.** This guide is published for the second of those, with the first treated as a bonus that probably will not arrive.

**Recommended `primaryQuery`:** `האפליקציה שבניתי עם AI הסתבכה מה עושים`

That is the reader's own words from the group, with the AI context added — the exact qualifier whose absence made Google read the bare phrase as a phone problem (#9). **It was not itself read live.** It is the honest target this page is written against, labelled as untested rather than dressed up as a verified opening.
