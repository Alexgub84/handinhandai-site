**One correction the fact-check forced, recorded here because the demand map's own number did not survive verification.** The `~₪1,500-2,000 + VAT` range above is not reproducible as a *lawyer's* fee. See the fact-check document: the only ₪2,000 that could be tied to a page is an online document service's fixed product price, on a page that also displays the same figure for registering a limited company and that partly failed to load. No published hourly or fixed rate for an Israeli lawyer drafting these two documents for a small product was found anywhere. **The guide therefore states no lawyer fee at all, and says in its own words that it found none and will not invent one.**

### Why write into a CROWDED field at all

Because the incumbents are the sellers. Every page that ranks is written by someone whose revenue depends on the reader buying a document, and none of the ones the map names answers the two questions the room is actually asking: *is it required at all*, and *what part can I do myself*. The three things this guide can argue that no seller's page will:

1. **Only one of the two documents is demanded by anybody**, and it is not the one people assume. Both stores require a privacy policy, in two places; neither page read requires an app to have its own terms-of-use document, and Apple publishes a standard EULA that applies unless a custom one is provided.
2. **The registration folklore is mostly dead.** Amendment 13 came into force 14 Aug 2025 and narrowed private-sector database registration to data traders at 10,000 people and up, plus public bodies. The room still asks whether it has to register a database. It almost certainly does not.
3. **The enforcement that actually happened was about content, not absence** — a ₪12,000 sanction where the documents existed but did not contain what section 11 requires. That is the exact failure mode of a copied template, and it is the one argument that beats "just copy one".

The fourth thing, and the one the work order named as what this guide owes the reader, is not an argument but a section: **what to have ready before calling a lawyer** — the seven technical facts only the builder knows, which are also the whole content of the document. That is first-hand ground, it is what cuts a billed hour, and no seller's page has a reason to publish it.
