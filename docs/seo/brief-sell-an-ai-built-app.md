### Facts verified in this session (2026-11-25), and how

Each of these was read off the owner's own page in this session by page fetch. They are the only numbers the guide states.

1. **Apple's reduced commission is 15%** on paid apps and Apple in-app purchases, for a developer with up to 1 million USD in proceeds in the prior calendar year and for a developer new to the store — [App Store Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/). Above that threshold the page says only "the standard commission rate will apply"; **it does not name that rate**, so the guide does not either.
2. **Google Play's service fee is not a single number, and depends on the transacting user's region** — [Service fees](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622). For users in Australia, the EEA, Japan, the UK or the US a new structure applies from a stated regional rollout date (30 June 2026 for the EEA, UK and US; 30 September 2026 for Australia and Japan): auto-renewing subscriptions 10% plus a 5% billing fee; other transactions 20% + 5% for a new install, 25% + 5% for an existing one (or 20% for external web links). For all remaining markets — which today includes a buyer in Israel — 15% for the first 1 million USD of annual earnings **for a developer enrolled in the 15% service-fee tier**, 30% above it, and 15% for auto-renewing subscriptions regardless of earnings.
3. **"עוסק פטור" is defined as a dealer whose turnover across all their businesses does not exceed ₪122,833 a year**, effective 1 Jan 2026 — the Israel Tax Authority's own [סכומים ושיעורים במע"מ](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/vat-rate-amount-new) (page updated 01.07.2026). **That page does not state the VAT rate itself**, so the guide quotes no VAT rate.
4. **Developer-account prices** are re-used from the site's already-published, same-dated checks: [$25 once on Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435) and [$99 a year at Apple](https://developer.apple.com/programs/).

### Who already ranks, and what only this desk can argue

Nobody owns the Hebrew version of this subject, so the comparison is with the English-sourced AI answer, which is a tool list: billing providers and project marketplaces. What it does not do is ask the question that decides everything before a tool is picked — **can this product be handed to anyone at all**. That is first-hand ground: who holds the code, the accounts and the domain; whether the product can run anywhere but the tool it was born in; and whether anyone other than the model can read the project. The last of those is the one nobody writes, and it is the price difference in a real handover.
